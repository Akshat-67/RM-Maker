How Auto-Renaming & Pairing Will Work:

  1. Auto-Pairing: Uploading name_front.jpg and name_back.jpg will link them as a pair automatically based on matching prefix + front/back keywords.
  2. Role Mapping Name: On clicking Apply Assignments, the app matches the extracted Aadhaar ID/name fuzzy-style against filenames. The tab changes from 1234_front.jpg to Borrower 1 (Name) - Aadhaar [Front].
  3. Persistence: These labels will refresh dynamically when loading the case step 2 page from session data, maintaining clean tabs.
